Hamare Baare Mein

Taleem ke saath tarbiyat bhi

Government High School 59\TDA ki bunyad is yaqeen par rakhi gayi ke har bacha seekh sakta hai, agar usay sahi mahaul aur sahi rehnumai mile.

Hum National Curriculum ke mutabiq taleem dete hain aur sath hi Islamiat, Urdu aur akhlaqiyat par khaas tawajjo dete hain. Har class mein bachay par infiradi nazar rakhi jaati hai taake koi bacha peechay na rahe.

Hamari koshish hai ke bacha Matric tak sirf achhay numbers hi nahi, balke ba-aitemaad, zimmedar aur ba-akhlaq shehri ban kar nikle.

Hamara Mission

Muayyari taleem, Islami aqdar aur jadeed maharatein ek hi chhat ke neeche dena.

Hamara Vision

Aisi nasl tayyar karna jo ilm, imaandari aur khidmat se mulk ka naam roshan kare.

Aam Sawalaat

      
Walidain aksar kya poochte hain

    

    

      
Kya saal ke darmiyan mein admission mil sakta hai?
Jahan class mein jagah ho wahan haan. Seat ki dastiyabi office se pooch lein.

      
Entry test mein kya poocha jata hai?
Class ke mutabiq English, Urdu aur Maths ki buniyadi maloomat. Chhoti classon mein sirf rang, shakalein aur chhoti guftagu hoti hai.

      
Kya fee qiston mein ada ho sakti hai?
Mahana fee har maah ki 10 tareekh tak ada hoti hai. Salana charges do qiston mein ada kiye ja sakte hain.

      
Kya yahan scholarship milti hai?
Honhaar aur zarooratmand talba ke liye scholarship hai. Mukammal tafseel office se maloom karein.

      
Transport kin ilaqon mein chalti hai?
Shehar ke bare ilaqon mein routes hain. Aapka ilaqa shamil hai ya nahi, form mein pata likh dein, office rabta karegi.

      
Parents-teacher meeting kab hoti hai?
Har imtihan ke baad result card ke saath PTM hoti hai. Zaroorat par kisi bhi din waqt le sakte hain.
